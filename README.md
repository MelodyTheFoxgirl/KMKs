# KMKs
Melody's Keymaster's Keep implementations, mostly focusing on rhythm games, specifically the Rhythm Heaven series and such.

## Games List
### Rhythm Heaven Megamix (3DS)
Defaults to Earth World and Heaven World enabled, but Challenge Land, Shop Games, and no-miss runs can also be enabled. If no levels are enabled, Earth World is forced on
#### What objectives are included?
Clearing individual games with a certain grade (and possibly getting the Skill Star) or a certain amount of Flow, completing an entire land/tower's worth of games, completing a Gatekeeper's trial, or completing a Challenge Course (if enabled)
#### Which objectives are considered difficult?
Paprika trials, Saltwater's Super Hard challenges, all of Paprika's challenges, and all no-miss runs
#### Which objectives are considered time-consuming?
No-miss runs and challenges with five or more steps
### Rhythm Heaven Groove (SW)
Defaults to Frontside and Flipside enabled, but Side Games, perfects, and night mode runs can also be enabled. If the Flipside is disabled, the Frontside will always be on, even if you have Side Games enabled
#### What objectives are included?
Achieving a certain grade in a minigame, getting a Perfect (if enabled), each of the preceding objectives but in Night Mode (if enabled), or doing a task in a side mode (if enabled): perform a certain number of correct actions in Rhythm Toy Box toys, complete a Beatspell chapter, play a Score Attack game, complete a Drum Lesson, or spend a few minutes jamming in a Soundboard in Rhythm Toy Box or Free Jam in Drum Lessons.
#### Which objectives are considered difficult?
Perfect attempts, anything involving Night Mode, and advanced Drum Lessons
#### Which objectives are considered time-consuming?
Perfect attempts, later Beatspell chapters, and all Drum Lessons
